Our Love Story

How We Met

It was an ordinary afternoon that turned extraordinary. We crossed paths at a mutual gathering, and something in the air felt different — a warmth, a quiet recognition, like two souls finally finding their way home.

First Conversation

Hours passed like minutes. We talked about everything and nothing — dreams, laughter, favourite monsoon memories. That first conversation became the thread from which our entire story was woven.

The Proposal

Under a sky full of stars, with marigolds and soft candlelight, came a question asked with trembling hands and a full heart. The answer was an instant, tearful, joyous yes that echoed into forever.
